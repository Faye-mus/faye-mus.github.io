Media artist and interaction designer building rooms, objects and stages that listen back. 媒体艺术家与交互设计师，创作会“回应”观众的空间、物件与舞台。

I'm Feihong Yu. I work where sound, light and the body meet: interactive installations, real-time visuals for performance, and small electronic objects you can hold. My projects move between Arduino and ESP32 hardware, Max and SuperCollider patches, and Unreal Engine scenes.

I'm currently part of the KIX Lab community at Indiana University, collaborating with composers, psychologists and filmmakers.

我是喻飞鸿（Feihong Yu）。我的创作位于声音、光与身体的交汇处：互动装置、为表演设计的实时影像，以及可以握在手中的小型电子物件。我的项目在 Arduino 与 ESP32 硬件、Max 与 SuperCollider 程序、以及 Unreal Engine 场景之间穿梭。

目前我在印第安纳大学 KIX Lab 的创作社群中，与作曲家、心理学研究者和电影人合作。
