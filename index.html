Feihong Yu

喻飞鸿

Feihong Yu is a composer, performer, and singer. She is currently pursuing her Master’s degree in Classical Voice and Computer Music Composition at the Indiana University Jacobs School of Music, where she is expanding her artistic practice of opera performance and technology-based composition.喻飞鸿是一位作曲家、表演者与歌者，目前在印第安纳大学雅各布斯音乐学院攻读古典声乐与计算机音乐作曲硕士，继续探索歌剧表演与以技术为媒介的作曲。

Her creative work includes compositions for voice and live electronics, interactive dance works using posture-capture systems, and electroacoustic sound design. Her work has been performed at multiple conferences, including EMM, MOXsonic, SEAMUS, and Musicacoustica Hangzhou.她的创作包括人声与现场电子音乐作品、运用姿态捕捉系统的互动舞蹈作品，以及电声设计。她的作品曾在 EMM、MOXsonic、SEAMUS 与 Musicacoustica 杭州等活动中呈现。

Feihong previously earned her Bachelor’s degree in Classical Voice from the Manhattan School of Music. As a performer, she has sung leading roles in operas throughout Italy and Germany and appeared in concerts across New York City and her hometown of Dalian, China.她在曼哈顿音乐学院获得古典声乐学士学位，曾在意大利和德国的歌剧制作中担任主要角色，也曾在纽约及家乡中国大连的音乐会上演出。

In addition to her performance and compositional pursuits, she has extensive experience in music production, having worked as an audio technician specializing in recording, mixing, and mastering.除了表演与作曲，她也有丰富的音乐制作经验，曾担任音频技术人员，从事录音、混音与母带制作。
